## Placement notes for the JLC review

- **CPL rotations**: JLC places the LCSC / EasyEDA footprint of each C-number, rotated counter-clockwise by the CPL
  rotation about its origin. Every row was fitted and then overlaid exactly as JLC transforms it (`sbc1-cpl-qa.txt`):
  **199 PASS, 0 FAIL, 9 CONFIRM**. The check requires the SAME pad (its number, or for shells / mounting / tactile
  pairs a pad of the same net). Diode polarity is checked by pin NAMES: the LCSC symbol's anode pad must land on our
  anode net and its cathode pad on our cathode net (our A / K from the schematic netlist); the polarity dot is reported
  per D line, and decides only where the LCSC pins carry no A / K names (D1 SMBJ5.0A: dot on our cathode pad 1).
  `scripts/test_fab_polarity.py` shows the check FAILs a reversed diode (CPL + 180, board A / K swapped, LCSC names
  swapped) for C2286, C12624, C84268 and C83333.
- **LEDs**: D2 (red) = Hubei KENTO KT-0603R C2286 (basic), D4 (yellow) = NationStar NCD0603Y1 C84268, D3 / D5 (green) =
  KENTO KT-0603G C12624. All pass by pin names. **D2's LCSC dot marks the ANODE**: C2286's LCSC pad 1 is named A (KENTO's
  drawing: pad 1 = '+') and carries the layer-101 dot; CPL rotation 0 puts LCSC pad 1 (A) on our pad 2 (LED_FAULT_A,
  x 104.79) and LCSC pad 2 (K) on our pad 1 (PWR_FAULT_N, x 103.21, the end with the silk cathode bracket). So in JLC's
  preview D2's dot sits on the end AWAY from our silk bracket - correct for this part (order remark above). For C84268
  and C12624 the dot marks the cathode and lands on our pad 1 (bracket end).
  D2 changed from NationStar NCD0603R1 C84263 (release-b) because JLC offers that part for Standard PCBA only.
- **J10 (HRO TYPE-C-31-D-15, C22464480)**: no LCSC footprint exists (EasyEDA API 404), so the footprint is drawn from
  HRO's 2023-10-27 drawing (`datasheets/dl/j10/C22464480.pdf`), rotated so the A row (A1 left) faces the board's top
  edge like the part it replaced. Its 4 shell legs (2.20 mm) pass through the 1.6 mm board in plated slots; at the two
  diagonal corners the slot also takes the dia 0.70 locating peg (the drawing leaves only a 0.135 mm web between slot
  and peg hole, which no copper ring survives).
- **J21-J28 (C588833)**: no LCSC footprint; CPL Mid = the jack body (courtyard) centre, not the KiCad origin on the
  peg line; plug opening toward the board's bottom edge (the stock orientation).
- **J9 (micro-B)**: its shell slots carry paste from the overlapping SMD shell pads (pin-in-paste, as drawn by
  Amphenol / KiCad) - keep it.
- THT connectors (J1, J4, J5, J6, J7, J8, J21-J28; the J9 / J10 shells) are JLC-placed too (one order = the full
  board).

## Edge clearance (order-time item)

JLC's assembly terms ask for component bodies >= 2.5 mm from the edge. On this board the harness connectors sit at the
stock positions (fixed by the cabinet harness) and overhang or touch the edges: J1 / J4 / J8 / J9 overhang the top /
right / left edges, J6 / J7 / J21-J28 are within 0.6 mm, SW1 / SW2 1.35 mm. Standard edge rails would be blocked by the
overhangs. If JLC asks for rails or a fixture, answer by asking them to assemble it as a single board.
